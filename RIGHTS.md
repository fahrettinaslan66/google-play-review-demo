No third-party television streams, movie or series clips, broadcaster logos, third-party music, copyrighted film artwork, or third-party footage are included.

The movie and series entries deliberately reuse synthetic test media and artwork. Their purpose is to let reviewers open every major section of the application without relying on third-party licensed content.
